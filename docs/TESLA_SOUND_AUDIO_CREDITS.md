# Tesla Sound X Studio V2 — Audio credits and restrictions

The app plays **original recordings** hosted on Wikimedia Commons. Its playback-rate setting changes the sound in the browser (no edited media file is distributed by the app). This is a demonstration system, **not a calibrated multi-RPM sound engine**.

## Porsche Taycan exact Electric Sport Sound

Porsche publishes an official audible example:
https://newsroom.porsche.com/en_US/2022/products/porsche-sound-of-the-taycan-christophorus-402-27910.html

The app does **not** host or claim to play Porsche's OEM Taycan sound by default, as permission to redistribute that OEM sample has not been established. A user may locally import a recording they are licensed to use; that file is not uploaded to our server.

## External speakers

Tesla's official Boombox (on vehicles with Pedestrian Warning System) can play current media externally in Park if the car offers the setting. A web page cannot directly select the pedestrian speaker, and operation of Boombox sounds while in Drive, Reverse, or Neutral has been restricted through an official recall/software update:
- https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/modely/en_hk/GUID-79A49D40-A028-435B-A7F6-8E48846AB9E9.html
- https://www.tesla.com/support/recall-disable-boombox

The app sends no commands to the vehicle. Real physical accelerator telemetry is **not connected**.
